The Method

1. The Brain Dump

Todayscape notebook system — one page captures every open loop, thought, and obligation.

2. Priority Matrix

Hand-drawn urgency/importance matrix to separate what matters from what feels urgent.

3. Time Boxing on Paper

A blank agenda divided into protected blocks. No digital calendar means no double-booking, no pop-ups.

4. Reflection Ritual

A 5-minute daily closeout: what stuck, what didn't, and one priority for tomorrow. Builds the muscle over time.
